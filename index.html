01 About

An engineer at the intersection of AI, vision & security.

// bio

AI & cybersecurity engineer designing, developing and deploying intelligent, secure, scalable ML systems for real industrial environments.

My work centers on high-performance deep-learning models — CNNs and YOLO-based architectures — for defect detection, object classification, and automated inspection across manufacturing and production workflows.

I combine classical computer-vision techniques (edge detection, feature extraction, contour analysis, color filtering) with model quantization and hardware-aware deployment to ship low-latency inference on NVIDIA Jetson and Raspberry Pi. Trained in cyber law and secure system design; I treat hardening as part of the pipeline, not an afterthought.
